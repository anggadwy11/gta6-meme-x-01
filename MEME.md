# The meme: "Statement of Account — Call Record" — a Leonida Telecom phone bill

## The joke
On the day Grand Theft Auto V launched — Tuesday, September 17, 2013 — a fan called
Leonida Telecom to ask about the sequel, and was put on hold. The call ended on Grand
Theft Auto VI's launch day, Thursday, November 19, 2026, when an agent finally
answered. The statement records the damage deadpan: one call, 4,811 days on hold, and
the hold announcement — "Your call is important to us" — played 1,731,960 times. It
was important. Eventually. His AI agent settles the bill from its make.fun bounty
earnings: the agent has unlimited patience, and unlike the phone company, it actually
delivers.

## The maths (all asserted in build_memes2.py with Decimal)
- Days: Sep 17, 2013 → Nov 19, 2026 = 4,811 days exactly (the account № LT-004811).
- Minutes on hold: 4,811 × 1,440 = 6,927,840 minutes.
- Local call: 6,927,840 min × $0.01 = $69,278.40.
- Hold-music licensing (13 yrs): waived. Patience surcharge: $0.00 — he had plenty.
- **TOTAL DUE: $69,278.40 — PAID IN FULL**
