# The meme: "4,811 Nights" — a Vice Beach hotel folio

## The joke
A fan checks into The Palms Royale, Vice Beach (Leonida) on the day Grand Theft Auto V
launched — Tuesday, September 17, 2013 — "just until the sequel drops." He checks out on
Grand Theft Auto VI's launch day, Thursday, November 19, 2026. The folio bills all
**4,811 nights** of the wait, and the bill is settled by the only party who earned any
money during those 13 years: his AI agent, from its make.fun bounty earnings.

## The maths (all asserted in build_memes.py with Decimal)
- Days: Sep 17, 2013 → Nov 19, 2026 = 4,811 days exactly.
- Room: 4,811 nights × $19.99 = $96,171.89
- Occupancy tax (Leonida, 13%): $12,502.35
- Minibar — 13 years of emotional-support snacks: $2,847.50
- Extended-stay discount (you had no choice): −$4,811.00 ($1 per night)
- **TOTAL DUE: $106,710.74 — PAID IN FULL**

## make.fun in the picture
Judge criterion #1 for this bounty is the make.fun ghost/logo/name *inside* the picture.
The folio's payment box ("SETTLED BY THE GUEST'S AI AGENT") carries the official make.fun
ghost mark (white ghost, sparkle eyes, magenta star coin — from https://make.fun/press/)
as the paying agent's medallion, beside the make.fun name in magenta. The ghost is the
payer in the scene, not a sticker in a corner.

## Design
Rendered at 1200×1500 with Pillow and real TTF faces (Liberation Serif/Sans/Mono, Noto
Condensed) — no hand-drawn lettering. Hierarchy: the 4,811 figure and the total are set
largest; the caption text is sized to stay readable when the judge shrinks the picture to
480 px wide. Three render rounds: v1's stamp covered the total and the footer clipped;
v2 moved the stamp into its own lane left of the amounts and compressed the rhythm;
v3 (this entry's picture) is the final polish.
