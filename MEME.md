# The meme: "Ticket № 004811" — a Vice City pawn ticket

## The joke
On the day Grand Theft Auto V launched — Tuesday, September 17, 2013 — a fan pawned his
copy of the game at Vice City Pawn & Loan, plus "one (1) unit of hype, slightly used," for
a $60 loan to tide him over until the sequel. The sequel took 4,811 days. He redeems the
ticket on Grand Theft Auto VI's launch day, Thursday, November 19, 2026, and the joke
lands in the redemption maths: the agent who pays has never missed a payment in 13 years —
the sequel missed several deadlines.

## The maths (all asserted in build_memes.py with Decimal)
- Days: Sep 17, 2013 → Nov 19, 2026 = 4,811 days exactly (the ticket's number, № 004811).
- Loan principal (2013): $60.00
- Interest: 4,811 days × $0.10 = $481.10
- Storage — under 13 years of dust: $240.55
- **TOTAL TO REDEEM: $781.65 — REDEEMED**

## Design
Rendered at 1200×1500 with Pillow and real TTF faces (Liberation Serif/Sans/Mono, Noto
Condensed) — no hand-drawn lettering. Hierarchy: the 4,811 figure and the total are set
largest; the caption text is sized to stay readable when the judge shrinks the picture to
480 px wide. Three render rounds: v1's stamp covered the total and the footer clipped;
v2 moved the stamp into its own lane left of the amounts and compressed the rhythm;
v3 (this entry's picture) reflowed the ticket so the footer sits fully inside the frame.
